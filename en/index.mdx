**Moment SDK** is an SDK that detects the user's app usage context (Moment) in real time, so you can deliver ads/messages at the most appropriate moment and pay out cashback based on payment data.

## Products

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Real-time Targeting (X)" icon="bullseye" href="/en/dev-guide/x/intro">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Audience Targeting (Sigma)" icon="chart-pie" href="/en/dev-guide/sigma-targeting/product-intro">

  <Card title="Cashback" icon="coins" href="/en/dev-guide/cashback/product-intro">
