Glossary

- Host: a company that has integrated the Fairy cashback service
- User: the end user who makes purchases at shopping malls through the Fairy cashback service embedded in the host's app
- Shopping mall: shopping malls in the Fairy cashback service (11st, Temu, AliExpress, etc.)
- Earnings: the record of a purchase made by a user at a shopping mall
  - Earnings created: earnings confirmed on Fairy's side
  - Earnings canceled: the user canceled the purchase
  - Earnings confirmed: earnings finalized via purchase confirmation, delivery confirmation, etc.
  - Cash accrual: commission accrued against confirmed earnings
- SDK push notification: push notifications sent by the Fairy SDK via user-behavior recognition to encourage purchases
- Fairy web console: [https://console.moment.fairytech.ai/](https://console.moment.fairytech.ai/)
