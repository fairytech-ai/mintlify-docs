## Creation flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log into the console">
    Sign in with your partner account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Campaigns > New campaign">
    Navigate to **Campaigns** in the left menu and click **New campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter basic info">
    Set the campaign name, dates, and target product (X / Sigma / Cashback).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure targeting">
    Choose a Sigma cohort or interest cluster to define the audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure creative">
    Enter the notification title, body, and landing URL, then preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and start">
    Submit for review; once approved, the campaign starts on the scheduled date.
  </Step>
</Steps>
