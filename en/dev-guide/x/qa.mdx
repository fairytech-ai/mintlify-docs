## Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify SDK initialization">
    Right after app launch, check the logs to confirm `Moment.init` was called successfully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the service">
    After calling `Moment.start(context)`, verify logcat prints `Moment recognition started`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Is the service on?">
    Once the recognition service is active, a foreground-service notification appears in the status bar.

    - Note: dismissing this notification does not stop the recognition service.

## Common issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The service won't start">
    In Settings \> Apps \> Special access \> Usage access, make sure the permission is granted. If `Moment.isRunning(context)` returns `false`, the service won't proceed.

    <Frame>
      ![Image](/images/image-8.png)
    </Frame>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The service is on but no notifications arrive">
    - Cause 1: Notification permission
      - Verify the app has the notification permission granted.
    - Cause 2: Snooze
      - To prevent notification fatigue, a snooze (~12 hours) is applied.
      - Check whether you received the same notification (same app moment \+ same content) recently.
      - To reset snooze: clear the app's data. (Except Coupang.)
    - Cause 3: The entered app is not configured in the console
      - Notifications only fire for entry points configured in the console.
    - \[When using Fairy's legal consent\] Cause 4: Missing required legal consents
      - Check that all consents required to receive notifications have been granted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
