## Moment X

**Moment X** detects the user's app-usage context (Moment) in real time so you can deliver ads/messages at the most appropriate moment. It currently supports Android only.

## Highlights

- Built on on-device technology; everything runs inside the device.
- Recognizes app usage and contextual signals to deliver messages at a considerate moment.
- Designed for minimal battery and app-size impact.

## Overview

The Moment SDK requires two permissions to detect user app-usage context.

- **App usage access (**`PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS`**)** — A special permission the user must grant directly in system settings.
- **Notification permission (**`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`**)** — Must be requested at runtime on Android 13 (API 33) or above.

The integration mode depends on who manages the legal consent required to use the service, and is selected at the contract and planning stage.
