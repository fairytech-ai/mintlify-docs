## Sigma Audience Targeting

**Sigma** classifies users of the partner app by interests and behavior, letting you use them for **audience targeting**. It sharpens ad/marketing campaign precision and reduces wasted impressions.

## Highlights

- Automatic user classification into meaningful interest clusters
- Results are provided **anonymized**; no personal identifiers are collected.
- Reuses existing marketing consent, so no additional consent collection is required.
