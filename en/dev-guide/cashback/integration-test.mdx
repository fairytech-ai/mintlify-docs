## Step 2) Test cashback

Test that a purchase made via a cashback link accrues correctly.

You need to test the transaction-created notification after a real purchase, and whether the reward is credited or scheduled.

Each mall reports transactions on its own cadence, so test using the malls below.

**Coupang, Naver Plus Store, Gmarket** — confirmable within 5 minutes of purchase.
